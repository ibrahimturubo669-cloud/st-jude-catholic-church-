About Our Parish

Who we are, what we believe and how you can belong.

St. Jude Catholic Church is a welcoming community of the Catholic faithful. We gather around the Eucharist, grow in the Word of God, and serve one another and our neighbours through our ministries and outreach.

Our parish is named for St. Jude Thaddeus, the Apostle known as the patron of hope and of difficult causes. His example reminds us to keep praying, to keep trusting, and to keep serving, even when the road is hard.

"Hope does not disappoint." (Romans 5:5)

    Privacy & data use

    How St. Jude Catholic Church handles information you give through this website.

    What we collect

    Name, phone number and ministry when you register; prayer intentions you submit; and the amount and category when you prepare an offering. We collect only what we need.

    How we use it

    To keep parish records, send parish communication, and run ministries. Prayer intentions are shared publicly only if you tick the box and the team approves. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

    Payments

    This website does not store M-PESA PINs or card details. Payments are completed on your phone through M-PESA.

    Your rights

    Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Contact the parish office using the details in the Contact section.
